# The Problem

**Students today face fragmented tools and constant distraction.**

- Study notes scattered across multiple apps
- Career preparation siloed in different platforms
- Finance tracking ignored until crisis moments
- Social apps optimized to hold attention, not build outcomes
- No single source of truth for academic + career + personal growth

**The cost:** Students waste time context-switching, miss opportunities, experience burnout, and lose agency over their own growth.

# Business Model

Tier-based pricing with sustainable unit economics.

| Tier | Cost | Best For | Dax Access |
|------|------|----------|-----------|
| Free | Forever free | All students | Llama 8B—basic concept help, study summaries, general advice |
| Pro | ₹299/month | Placement seekers | DeepSeek Flash—unlimited resume reviews, mock interviews, career coaching |
| Max | ₹499/month | High achievers | DeepSeek Pro—advanced reasoning for complex career decisions, personalized roadmaps, priority responses |

**Why it works:** Dax is the value driver. Free users get basic AI support (no ads, no tracking). Pro/Max students pay for compute they use—each tier unlocks a more capable model. Pro gains unlimited AI coaching; Max gets frontier-model reasoning for nuanced career strategy. Free tier stays ad-free because Pro margins sustain it.
